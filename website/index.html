Reproducibility Network for Computational Biology

Advancing reliable and reproducible computational biology research

Core Mission

The Reproducibility Network for Computational Biology (RN4CB) is a peer-led, globally inclusive consortium serving as a forum and voice to advocate, educate, and set standards for open and reproducible research practices.

Our aim is to raise the quality and trustworthiness of research in computational biology, medical informatics, and related data science fields.



What we do

The network will be involved in the following activities to further the mission:

  • Host educational events and produce materials including workshops and tutorials.
  • Develop best practice guidelines, recommendations and policy.
  • Maintain a media and online presence incl newsletter, social media and website
  • Give awards for excellence.
  • Provide career development opportunities.
  • Provide funding initiatives (seed grants).
  • Co-operate with other organisations
